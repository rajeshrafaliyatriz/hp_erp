## The jobs (do each through the UI; verify through the API or the database READ-ONLY)

J1 open Signal Review, open a signal, dismiss one with a reason (a seeded `S3 CHECK` row no longer exists: pick a real dev signal and REOPEN it afterwards so tenant data is unchanged, or create your own marked signal if the app allows). J2 Leads: add a lead, search it, filter by product and origin, open it, see product, consent chips and the Evidence tab. J3 convert your lead (a lead with a real person name creates organisation, contact and opportunity; a lead whose last name is only the company name must create NO contact and say so). J4 open your organisation: Account 360 and its Evidence section, the contact page, accept one fact if a suggestion exists (else say there is none). J5 add a note and a task on the organisation; run the agent once (read what it proposes: do the sentences match the rows?). J6 move your opportunity through its stages on the board and in the form, close it Lost and look for a loss reason field (the S11a backend has `loss_reason_code`; the screens may not show it yet: that is a finding, not a failure of your script). J7 build a quote for your organisation from a product, turn it into a sales order and an invoice (stop before any send). J8 CRM Campaigns and GTM Campaigns: the CRM campaigns menu row is hidden for everyone, so say how a person gets there (the route `/module/crm/marketing/campaigns` directly) and whether it works; create a campaign, add your organisation as a target. J9 Outreach Approvals (also a hidden row): open `/module/crm/marketing/outreach-approvals` directly and say what a person sees. J10 ask the chat: "How many leads do I have and where did they come from?" and check the answer against the database. J11 Agent Run History: the three tabs, the Waiting tab, the Autonomy tab (look only; do not change a level). J12 Forecast and Reports: open both, say whether the numbers match a hand count of your marked opportunity.
