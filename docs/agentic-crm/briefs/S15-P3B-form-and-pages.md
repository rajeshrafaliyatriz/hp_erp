## Goal
A person adds, edits and reads ONE Sub-product (or Product) in one place, with every field of the old Products form and of the old Portfolio form, and the six tabs of both modules. You build the form and the pages; Track P3-A builds the list and the sidebar, Track P3-C the shared product picker and its consumers. **Words everywhere: Product and Sub-product (a service is a Sub-product of type Service). Never show the words Catalogue, Offer, Item or Product line** (a scan test enforces it on your files).

`components/domain/portfolio/product-form-drawer.tsx` exporting exactly:
```ts
export type ProductFormMode = 'create-sub-product' | 'create-product' | 'edit'
```

## What to build
1. **The form** (a drawer, `components/ui/sheet`, with a dirty-close guard; reuse `components/shared/scaffolds` where they fit: `record-drawer`, `form-layout`, `form-field`). Three sections as in mock-up view 3:
   - **About**: Product (select, required), ID (offer id, required on create, immutable after), Name, Readiness, and EVERY field of today's Portfolio offer form (`components/domain/portfolio/product-form-dialog.tsx`): what it does, features and capabilities (modules/components), problems it solves, target customers, buying events, benefits and proof, pricing model, deal size, deployment model, delivery owner, implementation effort, notes, plus the NEW control Often sold together (a multi-select of existing sub-products; API: `about.oftenSoldTogether`, offer ids; says "new" nowhere in the UI). Readiness confirmation stays the existing admin action, not in this form.
   - **Sell in CRM**: a switch that reveals EVERY field of today's Products form (`create-product-modal.tsx`, the Details tab of `product-detail-page.tsx`): type (Sub-product or Service), SKU, category (the `products.category` picklist), unit price, cost price, currency, tax rate, GL account, commission %, vendor, usage unit, stock, stock in demand, reorder level, weight (kg), image (upload after save), assigned to (defaults to the signed-in person), description on quotes. After creation the person sees ONE switch, **Offered on new documents** (on, or paused: nothing is deleted); the writer returns 422 on a contradiction, show it on that field. The Sell section appears only for a caller who may edit it (`can.viewSell`/`can.createSubProduct` from the API); HR sees About and Ideal customer only.
   - **Ideal customer**: the three ICP tabs exactly as in today's Portfolio form.
   One Save writes everything through `POST /api/product-master` or `PUT /api/product-master/{id}` (one transaction; field-path 422 errors shown on their fields; a 403 names the refused section); image upload, custom fields and bundle items are saved right after through their existing endpoints (keyed by the returned `sell.crmProductId`) with a visible status per section, never a silent failure. **Add product (level 1)** (mode `create-product`, administrators): name and key only, as `new-product-dialog.tsx` does today (`kind=product`).
2. **The Sub-product page** `/portfolio/products/[id]` (new route; the existing `app/portfolio/[product]` stays the Product page): header with name, Product, state chips (Sold in CRM / Paused / Not sold, readiness, signals count), Edit (opens the drawer), Archive and Restore, **Delete from catalogue** (administrators: moves the CRM row to the Recycle Bin through `DELETE /api/crm/products/{crmId}`; the Sub-product stays as Not sold; confirm first), and the six tabs: **Overview** (the About, positioning, readiness with the admin confirmation as today), **Details** (the Sell data: every field, bundle-aware; hidden for HR), **Bundle** (priced components with quantity: the old `AddBundleComponentDialog` and the three bundle endpoints; products only, as today), **Usage** (counts and the latest quotes, orders and invoices from `GET /{id}/usage`, each linking to its document), **History** (`GET /{id}/history`, Sell lines only for a viewer who may see them), **Signals & ICP** (the signals found for it and the ICP editor/summary; reuse `product-signals-icp-tab.tsx` and the Portfolio ICP components) + **Custom Fields** only when the record has a CRM row and the organisation has definitions. Tabs the caller may not see are not drawn (no empty tab, no 403 text). The page reads `GET /{id}` and the tab data lazily when a tab opens. A record that is a level-1 Product opens the Product page, not this one.
3. **The Product page** (`app/portfolio/[product]`, today Overview + Offers): tabs **Overview**, **Sub-products** (replaces "Offers": a paged list linking to each Sub-product page, with Add sub-product), **Signals & ICP**; keep what the page does today (setup steps, profile panel) and its source-scan pins unless you rewrite them on purpose.
4. **Old addresses**: the page `/portfolio/products/[id]` also accepts being reached through `GET /by-crm/{crmId}` redirects owned by Track P3-A; you do not write those.
5. Add `products` to `RESERVED_KEYS` in `lib/product-key.ts` (and its test), so a Product key can never be `products`.
