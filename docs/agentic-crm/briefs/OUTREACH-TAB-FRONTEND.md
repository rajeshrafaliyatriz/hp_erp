## What the tab is

A fourth tab "Outreach" of Market Signals (`/signals/outreach`), after Product Portfolio. Companies that signals found (or a person added by hand) and the people at them; the user can write to them. The design COPIES the Organizations module's list pages (read `components/domain/crm/organization-list-view.tsx`, `org-scopes.tsx`, `people-table.tsx`, `organization-detail-page.tsx`, `components/domain/crm/people/*`, `lib/crm/people.ts`, `lib/org-accounts/*`) but is its own set of components on the new API; **never edit any Organizations file**. Reuse generic pieces directly: `components/shared/scaffolds/{page-tabs,use-url-tab}` (`PageTabs`, `TabPanel`, `useUrlTab`), `SectionCard`/`ReadField` from `components/shared/business/shared`, `useBulkSelection`, `CrmBulkActionBar`, `PersonRail`, `who-chip`, `lib/org-accounts/tones` where their props allow; copy a component only where it is bound to the CRM API.

Views of the list (`?view=` = `companies | people | messages`, `OUTREACH_VIEWS`): **Companies**, **All people**, **Messages**. The company page has exactly two tabs: **Details** and **People** (`COMPANY_TABS`, `?tab=`).

Data honesty: contacts found on the public web are unverified; their rows say "Found on a public page, not verified" with the source link. Reach words and tones come from `lib/signals/outreach-model.ts` (`REACH_LABEL`, `REACH_TONE`, `REACH_HELP`); filter chips from `OUTREACH_CHIPS` with numbers from the server `summary` (`chipCount`); the query a chip means from `outreachQuery`/`withChange`.

Empty states must tell a person what to do next ("No companies yet. Generate signals in Market Signals, or add one by hand.", "No contact found for this company. Find contacts, or add one by hand.").
