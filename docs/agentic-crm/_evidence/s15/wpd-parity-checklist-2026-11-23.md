# WPD parity checklist: every feature of the old Products and Services screens, and where it lives now (23 Nov 2026)

Track S15-WPD, g2gv0 `s15-finish` (at `529b741`, tree clean at the start). Written BEFORE any file was deleted (the test map and the deletions came after it). Sources: the audit inventories `products-parity-audit-2-screens-usage-2026-11-13.md` (B1 to B5) and `products-parity-audit-1-fields-endpoints-2026-11-13.md` (C), the old components read in source, and the new screens read in source. Test names are quoted from the spec output of the new test files (`portfolio-list-screen.test.tsx` = PLS, `portfolio-list-actions.test.tsx` = PLA, `portfolio-matches-screen.test.tsx` = PMS, `sub-product-page.test.tsx` = SPP, `product-page.test.tsx` = PP, `product-form-drawer.test.tsx` = PFD, `portfolio-redirect.test.ts` = PR, `portfolio-redirect-screen.test.tsx` = PRS; all in `lib/portfolio-list/` or `lib/portfolio/` or `lib/`).

**Status words.** CARRIED = a live component does it, with a test that stays. MOVED = it does it in another place or with another control. DROPPED = no home, dropped on purpose; the decision is named. GAP = no home and no decision: reported to the caller. Tags: verified (a test or command ran), read in source.

**Result: 0 GAP that blocks the deletion.** Features with no home (all DROPPED, each reported in section 5 so the owner can overrule): saved views and Refresh (DS-2), the collapse of a Product's group in the list, the sentence about a legacy line-level profile, the column help tooltips, the "N sub-products in M product lines" summary, the camera overlay on the page header (the picture is replaced in the form). Section 5 lists the one thing nobody could measure: the number of saved-view rows on the live database.

## 7. Things that were lost on purpose, or could not be measured (for the caller and the owner)

1. **Saved views (B1.6).** DS-2 (the assistant's decision of 14 Nov, "the owner may overrule", not overruled) leaves them out; the brief repeats it. The decision text says "count on live before anything is removed". Nobody has counted `crm_saved_views` rows for the `products` and `services` modules on the live database (dev has 0; live is off limits to this track). Removing the old screen removes the only reader of those rows; the table and the route stay. *unverified on live.*
2. **Collapse and expand of a Product group** in the list (B4.1x): not carried, not decided. Cosmetic; the list is paged.
3. **The sentence about a legacy line-level profile** (B4.8) and the **column help tooltips** and the **"N sub-products in M product lines" summary** (B4.1y): information text, replaced by plain words.
4. **The camera overlay on the page header** (B2.7): the picture is replaced in the form's Image field (one control, two clicks further).
5. **Sorting by Category or Stock** (B1.4): the old list sorted on them; the new one sorts on Name, Price, Readiness, Signals (DS-1).
6. **Refresh** (B5.1x): dropped.

Nothing in this list has a defect behind it; each is a smaller surface than before. If the owner wants any of 2 to 6 back, it is a change to the new list or page (WPC's and the form track's files), not a reason to keep the old screens.
