What each tab holds

  • Overview: what it does, problems solved, segments, buying events, readiness (from the Portfolio)
  • Details: SKU, prices, tax, GL, stock (from Products)
  • Bundle: priced parts of a bundle, with quantity (from Products)
  • Usage: which quotes, orders, invoices, price books and signals use it
  • History: every change, with who and when (now includes About edits)
  • Signals & ICP: signals found for it and its ideal customer
  • Custom Fields appears only when your organisation has some

A person without the Products right (for example HR) sees Overview and Signals & ICP only: no price, cost, commission, stock, bundle or history.

A Product page (ScholarClone) has three tabs: Overview, Sub-products, Signals & ICP.
