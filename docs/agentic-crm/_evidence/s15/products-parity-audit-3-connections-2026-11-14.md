## 1. Summary

**Counts (full table in section G5):** D 29 items (24 OK, 3 GAP rows, 1 DECISION, 1 INCOMPLETE); E 62 items (49 OK, 8 GAP rows, 5 DECISION rows, 0 INCOMPLETE). Together 91 items: 73 OK, 11 GAP rows that are **7 distinct findings**, 6 DECISION rows that are **6 distinct decisions**, 1 INCOMPLETE row plus 2 open measurements.

**What holds up (verified).** Every path that writes a product store was found by grepping the four table names and the model classes over `app`, `database/seeders` and `routes` (65 files). All HTTP doors (`crm/products*`, `portfolio/offers*`, `portfolio/import`, `signals/icp/products*`, `product-master*`) keep their routes and answers and mirror to the master row; the checker `product-master:check` is clean on dev today (22 pointers, 20 tables, 64 master rows, 9 live items, 50 offers, every invariant 0). Signals research, Ask, the GTM agents, lead conversion, the quote and invoice PDFs, every email or automation token and every notification and job read or write only keys that no step of the plan changes (the offer's text id, the product key, `crm_products.id`, the master id of a Product). The 12 plus 10 pointer list is complete for its two targets: `information_schema` shows no other column at `crm_products.id` or `g2g_product_master.id`.

**Top gaps (one line each; detail, size and plan step in section G1):**
1. **G-E1 (M)** Archiving a Sub-product changes only the master row; matching, research, ICP offer lists, `/portfolio/products` and the old Portfolio ignore it, and P3-A and P3-B draw the Archive action and the Archived filter. Blocks the Archive control.
2. **G-D2 (S)** `portfolio:import-offers`, `g2g:import-portfolio` and `G2GPortfolioSeeder` write offers with no mirror; the new list starts from master rows, so their rows are invisible until `product-master:repair`; `--replace` bypasses the D-8 refusal. Blocks the cutover if `portfolio:import-offers` carries offers to live.
3. **G-D1 (S)** The checker is the only net under five doors and nothing schedules it; the legacy mirror swallows its own failure.
4. **G-E2 (S)** The pointer registry has no family for pointers at the About row (`g2g_offer_readiness_log.offer_row_id`, the text offer ids) and its name pattern cannot see a new one.
5. **D-E4 (S, decision)** Product-level facets are exact matches while signals and the validators may hold a Sub-product id.
6. **G-E4 (S)** The "alias for a retired id" has no reader; the claims check still scans the archived husk's name.
7. **G-E6 (S)** Ask's `list_products` speaks the retired words and the old addresses.
8. **G-E7 (S)** The Signals product selector, two `options()` callers and the asset import column sit outside P3-A, P3-B and P3-C.
9. **D-E1 (S, decision)** HR cannot archive or restore a Sub-product (gated by the Products row).
10. **D-E2 (S, decision)** Ask's `list_products` needs the Products row, so HR is refused names the Portfolio shows.

**Pointer columns the registry misses:** none at the two registered targets. One numeric pointer at the OTHER store is outside it: **`g2g_offer_readiness_log.offer_row_id`** (at `g2g_product_offers.id`, 0 rows on dev), plus text-id references to offers in signals, runs, matches, ICP configs, `bundles_with` and `offers_authorized` (section E-C).

**Wrong in the plan:** see G3 (the "alias for a retired id", "mirrors in the same transaction" for legacy doors, "Archived" as a way to leave use, "reads open to any signed-in user" versus Ask and archive, "forms point at level 1" as a rule).

**Could not verify:** live (nothing read), a real MySQL run of the writer, any browser behaviour, PHPUnit (read only), the uncommitted files may change (G4).
