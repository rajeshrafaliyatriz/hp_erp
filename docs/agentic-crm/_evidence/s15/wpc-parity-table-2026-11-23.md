## D. The pair review (the duplicate pairs the owner leaves for the new screen)

Administrators only; the verdict, score reasons and usage as the server sends them; "Same product" asks first and names both records; "Different" is remembered and the pair does not come back; every decision reads the list and the pairs again. Proved on a fixture copied from the REAL dry run of 13 Nov (nine items, two EDITION pairs sharing one Sub-product, two AMBIGUOUS, two services, three no-match): `lib/portfolio-list/portfolio-matches-real.test.tsx` (14 tests) and the earlier `portfolio-matches-screen.test.tsx` (19). Mutations R1 to R7 and M22, M31, M32.

## E. What is NOT carried over (and why), and what is still owed

- Saved views and the Refresh button: dropped on purpose (above). The live row count of `crm_saved_views` is the owner's check before the table goes.
- Sorting by Category or Qty In Stock: dropped on purpose (DS-1).
- The product number in the search: waits for WPA1's `product_no` search.
- Nothing in B1 has no home. B2 (the old detail page), B3 (the create and edit flows) and B5.3 to B5.17 belong to Track P3-B's Sub-product and Product pages (see `p3b-parity-table-2026-11-14.txt`) and to Track WPD's removal list.
