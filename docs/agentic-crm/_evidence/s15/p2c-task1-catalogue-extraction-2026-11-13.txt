S15 P2-C task 1: the catalogue field map and rules moved out of CrmProductController into App\Domain\ProductMaster\CatalogueFields (13 Nov 2026)

What moved (CrmProductController, no change of behaviour):
  - payload()                          -> CatalogueFields::payload(array $input, bool $forUpdate, ?Closure $hasLineColumn)   (the controller's private payload() is now one line that calls it)
  - the six validation rules           -> CatalogueFields::rules(bool $forUpdate)   (name, assignedTo, unitPrice, costPrice, commissionRate, taxRateId, in the order the controller always had; store() and update() spread it first, then keep their own line rules)
  - item types                         -> CatalogueFields::ITEM_TYPES   (the controller's private const now points at it)
  - the PRD-/SVC- number               -> CatalogueFields::newProductNo()   (store() and import())
  - "a service never carries stock"    -> CatalogueFields::applyServiceRules()   (store(), update(), import())
The five other controller routes (index, show, destroy, merge, import's own row rules...) are untouched.
