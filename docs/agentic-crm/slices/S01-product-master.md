## Goal

One product record that every other branch (signal, campaign, lead, outreach, forecast) can point at. A thin index over the four existing stores. It is not a merge, so quotes, orders and invoices keep working.

## Why this is first

`crm_products`, `g2g_product_offers`, `g2g_signal_products` (plus `config/signals_products.php`) and `g2g_product_profiles` are four product stores with no link between them. GTM has no product model. Promoting a signal to a lead copies no product. Ten tables hold real foreign keys to `crm_products`. Source: Explore and Plan reports of 2026-11-02 (`agent-read`; re-verify in task 1).

**Design decisions of the fold-in** (3 Nov, the assistant's own, from the code; each has a reason; the owner may change them):

1. **One screen, two scopes.** The Products screen (`/module/crm/sales/products`, content-map entry `ProductListView`) opens on the Catalogue exactly as before. Under its title a two-button switch "Catalogue | Product lines" (`aria-pressed`, the pattern `ag-agent-library.tsx` already uses for its card and table views) opens the product lines in the SAME screen; the scope is `?scope=lines` in the address (the `useListQuery` scaffold), so refresh, Back and a pasted link keep it. Why not a tab strip: the catalogue list has no tabs and the S1 scaffolds' `PageTabs` are for record pages. The switch is shown only on Products (never Services: a service is not in a product line) and only where the server says the Product Master exists (`lines.available`), so a host without the S1 database (live today) shows the screen exactly as it was. While the product lines are open the switch always stays, so there is always a way back.
2. **The list's extra columns** (Product line, Signals, ICP) appear only when `lines.linked > 0`, i.e. at least one item of that type is tied to a sub-product. Why: on live and dev today the catalogue is empty or has no linked item, and three empty columns are noise. They apply to Services too (a sub-product can be sold as a service); the shared component handles both.
3. **Where a record opens.** A catalogue item (including a sold sub-product) opens its own catalogue page (`/products/{id}` or `/services/{id}`), which gains the tabs "Signals & ICP" and "Usage" when a record exists behind it (a sub-product gets both, a catalogue-only item gets Usage alone: it has no signals or ICP to show). A product line, or a sub-product that is not sold, has no catalogue page, so it opens `/module/crm/sales/products/lines/{id}` (a route under the Products row: no menu row, the same sidebar highlight and breadcrumb). A sold sub-product listed under its line links straight to its catalogue page, so every object has exactly one page. Why not `?record=` in the list route: the other CRM detail pages are real routes and the breadcrumb needs the record's name.
4. **Words.** On the Products screen "product" already means an item the CRM sells, so the top-level group is called a **product line** there (the S1 word "product" is kept in the API and the code); a sub-product keeps its name. Server messages that still say "product" for a line are unchanged (they are tested and shown verbatim).
5. **The S1 screens fold in, not copy.** The list became `product-lines-view.tsx` (the Product Master list minus its page frame, plus Signals and ICP per row and a catalogue-aware link per row); the record page became `product-line-record-page.tsx` (tabs Overview, Signals & ICP, Sell in CRM, Usage; the S1 Prices tab is part of Usage); the S1 drawer form is kept (`product-master-form.tsx`, with an optional `showSell` so the catalogue item's own page can edit a sub-product's details without a second place to edit its price). "New…" offers a product line and a sub-product only: a catalogue item is added with the existing "Add Product" and "Add Service" buttons, whose dialog is untouched. `services/product-master/*` and `lib/product-master/*` stay: the Signal Review screen (S2) reads the options through that service.
6. **Removed:** the standalone route `app/module/crm/product-master/`, the two S1 screen components, the content-map import and route lines (only those lines), and the navigation constant `CRM_PRODUCT_MASTER_ACCESS_LINK`.
7. **Rights.** The master API's rights are the Products row's (view, add, edit, delete), as built by the previous agent: routes are `platformright:/module/crm/sales/products,<action>` (the pattern the other CRM entities use; the CRM product routes themselves check inside the controller because two rows share one controller). A person with only the Services row cannot read the product lines.
