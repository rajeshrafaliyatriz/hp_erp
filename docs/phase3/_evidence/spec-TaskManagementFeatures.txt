Task Management — Role-Wise Feature List 
 
​
1. Admin 
●​ System Configuration: Define global task settings, priorities (e.g., Low, 
Medium, High, Urgent), and custom task statuses (e.g., To Do, In Progress, 
Review, Blocked, Done). 
●​ Access & Permissions: Assign roles, manage data visibility constraints, and 
configure access policies determining who can see or modify tasks across 
departments. 
●​ Integrations & Webhooks: Connect the module with external tooling (e.g., 
version control systems, communication channels, CRM pipelines) and 
automate data ingestion. 
●​ Audit Logging: Review comprehensive system logs to track task creation, 
deletion, or critical modifications for compliance and security auditing. 
2. Executives 
●​ Macro Dashboard Tracking: View aggregated, high-level analytics on overall 
company task completion rates, bottleneck alerts, and organizational velocity. 
●​ Cross-Functional Alignment: Monitor cross-departmental task 
dependencies that directly impact critical corporate milestones or strategic 
quarterly goals. 
●​ Strategic Escalations: Intervene in high-priority, blocked, or critically overdue 
tasks that present major operational or financial risks. 
3. Department Head 
●​ Workload & Capacity Management: Review task distribution across the 
department to balance workloads, prevent team burnout, and reassign tasks 
when resources are constrained. 
●​ Department-Wide Progress Audits: Monitor the status of all departmental 
projects, tracking timelines, milestones, and deliverables to ensure SLAs 
(Service Level Agreements) are met. 
●​ Cross-Departmental Dependencies: Coordinate and manage task handoffs, 
approvals, and dependencies with other functional heads. 
●​ Approval & Sign-off: Review and formally approve completed critical-path 
tasks or deliverables before they advance to the next operational phase. 
4. HR Manager 

●​ Performance & SLA Analytics: Analyze task completion rates, overdue 
frequencies, and operational efficiency metrics to support performance 
reviews and identify high performers. 
●​ Competency Identification: Map task performance and execution speeds 
against specific roles to identify functional skill gaps or emerging internal 
subject matter experts. 
●​ Onboarding & Compliance Oversight: Monitor specialized HR task tracks, 
such as standard operating procedure (SOP) training compliance, background 
checks, and automated employee onboarding sequences. 
5. Employee 
●​ Daily Task Execution: View, sort, and prioritize their assigned personal tasks 
using custom filters (e.g., sorting by Due Date, Urgency, or Project category). 
●​ Status & Progress Updates: Update task lifecycles in real-time (moving a 
task from In Progress to Review or Completed) and log time or effort spent. 
●​ Contextual Collaboration: Add technical comments, upload 
files/deliverables directly to the task card, and tag colleagues or managers for 
feedback. 
●​ Blocker Flagging: Mark a task as "Blocked" or "On Hold" and document the 
specific operational hurdles preventing execution to alert their Department 
Head.
