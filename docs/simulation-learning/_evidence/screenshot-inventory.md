# Feature inventory read from the 8 screenshots

Saved 2026-10-26 from the approved plan (the whole plan is in `_evidence/approved-plan-2026-10-26.md`). Claims are tagged verified, inferred or unverified in the source text.


**PhET Quantum Wave Interference (screenshot 1).** Apparatus view (source, barrier, detector, a 0.60 m dimension line, particle mass label); a close-up of the barrier with dimensioned slits (0.06 µm wide, 1.0 µm apart); a particle selector (photons, electrons, neutrons, helium); every input is a numeric box with units plus a slider and stepper arrows (speed 200 to 1000 m/s, emission rate, slit separation 0.1 to 2.0 µm, distance 0.40 to 0.80 m); a configuration dropdown; a detector screen with zoom, a scale bar, intensity or hits display, brightness, a collapsible intensity graph, a ruler, snapshot and gallery buttons, and a reset-all button; three tabs that raise the complexity (Experiment, High Intensity, Single Particles); a footer with home, settings, sound and keyboard-help controls. The pattern: direct manipulation, linked representations, units everywhere, implicit scaffolding, measuring tools.

**PhET Projectile Data Lab (screenshot 2).** Orientation and projectile pickers; six "mystery launchers" (hidden parameters to infer); a histogram with a bin-width selector, a sound icon and a switch between two histogram views; paths, launch-angle and launch-speed overlays; measuring tape and stopwatch; single or continuous firing; speed toggle; sample buttons 1 to 6; clear and reset; four tabs (Variability, Sources, Measures, Sampling). The pattern: an inquiry loop (launch, observe, build the distribution) with instruments.

**Cell Architecture Studio (screenshots 3 and 4).** A cell-type list with thumbnails; a hero 3D cross-section with organelles; view modes (solid, layers, points) and a cross-section toggle; a toolbar (rotate, isolate, hide others, reset view, screenshot, 3D export) and a sticky-note control hint (drag, scroll, Ctrl-drag); an organelle card (name, tagline, size, location, visible-in-light-microscope, label toggle, favourite); biological notes with a fun fact; a "where it occurs" body diagram; microscope images (light, stained, electron) with add-image; a compare-cells view; gallery, library and notebooks in the top bar; a warm paper palette with serif headings; staggered card transitions when switching cells (screenshot 4 catches one). Content check: the Lysosome card says "Size: About 7-20 µm" and "Location: Blood, lymph, and tissues", which describe the whole white blood cell, not the organelle.

**Learn Arduino (screenshots 5 and 6).** A left rail (home, lessons, components, simulate, settings, language); a progress chip (Basics 11/16); an isometric 3D scene (board, breadboard, coloured wires, an OLED showing "54% Happy :)", a potted plant with a moisture sensor, a watering can you press and hold; a stepper motor, push button and prize wheel in lesson 13); live value labels in the scene (A0 = 379; step 1089 / 2560); a context card with "zoom to it" and an interaction hint; a code editor with the running line highlighted and a stop button; a serial monitor with a "Compiling sketch / Uploading / Done uploading" log; a scene toolbar (undo, redo, zoom, reset view, signal view); a lesson carousel with completion ticks. The pattern: a closed loop between code, 3D objects and live readings, with tight guidance. Open question for Step 0: is the "compile" a real server call or scripted?

**Forge AI (screenshots 7 and 8, identical).** A 3D breadboard with an AI command bar: suggestion chips ("Add a power LED", "Double the blink rate", "Add a reset button", "Make it a monostable"), a free-text prompt, a model picker ("FORGE AI") and a send button. The pattern: plain-language edits to a structured scene.

**What this gives the runtime: a UI kit to build once** (parameter panel with number + slider + units; graph and histogram panels; code panel; console; object inspector and label toggles; compare view; lesson carousel with progress; hint panel; AI command bar with chips; snapshot and export; reset-all; keyboard help), plus the 3D controls (orbit, zoom, pan, isolate, cross-section, reset view).
