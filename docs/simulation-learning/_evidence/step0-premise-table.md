## Cell Architecture Studio: what the post and the 24 frames show

- **The post** (x.com/DilumSanjaya/status/2053155739389378849; 9 May 2026 16:50 UTC; 17,842 likes; 39.5 s video, 3024 x 1714): "Fun interactive science app ideas | Part 3. Played around with generating 3D biological structures and made an app to explore them interactively. UI Design: GPT Images 2. Code: Gemini 3.1 Pro. More demos."
- **Sequence:** seven cell types of about 5 s each (plant, white blood, neuron, epithelial, bacteria, animal, muscle); the stage turns slowly; the cross-section is on throughout; the accent colour changes per cell.
- **Layout, identical in every frame:** header (Gallery, Library, Notebooks, Settings, avatar); left: a cell-type list with thumbnails and an organelles list with colour dots; centre: serif title and subtitle, a sticky-note control tip, a three-icon view-mode switch, a cross-section toggle, the 3D stage, a toolbar (Rotate, Isolate, Hide Others, Reset View; Screenshot, 3D Export); right: an Organelle Details card (name, tagline, three facts, a label toggle, a colour dot, a favourite heart), Biological Notes (a paragraph and a fun fact), "Where it occurs" (a watercolour illustration); bottom: Microscope View (light, stained, electron, Add Image) and Compare Cells (this cell against another, Open Comparison View).
- **It is a data-driven template.** One layout is filled from a per-cell record (title, subtitle, accent colour, organelles, detail facts, notes, fun fact, illustration, microscope images, compare partner). That maps directly onto the manifest-plus-shell runtime in the plan.
- **Models:** painterly, illustrated textures; the neuron is nearly flat; the others are textured cutaways. Consistent with AI-generated meshes with painted textures [inferred].
- **Content accuracy (reading the cards):** the White Blood Cell's Lysosome card says "Size: About 7-20 µm", which is a whole-cell size (lysosomes are about 0.1-1.2 µm) [verified on screen; textbook value inferred]. The Bacteria nucleoid card says "Visible in LM: No, EM only", an oversimplification (nucleoids show under fluorescence light microscopy with DNA stains) [inferred]. The axon, microvilli, mitochondrion and myofibril cards look plausible. So AI-built content needs a named expert's review, which supports the plan's rule.
- **Not captured:** the fonts (a serif display face and a handwriting script; video only) and the real tech stack.
